# Monitor Switcher — Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: November 3, 2026**

## Data Collection

Monitor Switcher does not collect, store, share, or sell personal information or user data.

## How the Extension Works

Monitor Switcher uses Chrome's `system.display` and `windows` APIs to identify connected monitors, determine the position of the current browser window, and move that window between monitors.

All processing occurs locally within the user's browser. No information is transmitted to external servers.

## Third-Party Services

Monitor Switcher does not use third-party analytics, advertising services, or tracking technologies.

## Changes to This Privacy Policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the extension's functionality or data practices change. Any updates will be published on this page.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, users can contact the developer through the project's GitHub repository.
